PART 2: DETAILED MODULE-BY-MODULE SPECIFICATIONS & WORKFLOWS

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 01: Lead Management, Enquiry & Patient Relationship CRM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Purpose: Capture, nurture, and track initial couple enquiries from multiple touchpoints.
2. Inflow Channels: Website webhooks, WhatsApp Business API, walk-in reception, call centers, and physician referrals.
3. Key Functionality:
   - Couple Lead Profiling: Husband & Wife names, age, duration of marriage, primary/secondary infertility history, contact information.
   - Lead Status Stages: New Enquiry -> Contacted -> Preliminary Counseling Booked -> Seminar/Webinar Attended -> Initial Appointment Confirmed -> Dropped / Follow-up Needed.
   - Smart Auto-Assignment: Route leads to dedicated financial/clinical fertility counselors based on language or location.
   - Omnichannel Communication: Automated WhatsApp and SMS reminders for consultation dates, directions, and fasting/test guidelines.
   - Conversion Analytics: Cost-per-lead, source-to-consultation conversion rate, and dropout reason tracking.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 02: Couple Onboarding, Biometrics, Legal KYC & ART Act Compliance
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Purpose: Establish a single unified "Couple Profile" with stringent legal compliance and anti-mixup identity validation.
2. Data Structure:
   - Primary Entity: "Couple ID" linked to two individual profiles: Female Partner (UID-F) and Male Partner (UID-M).
   - Relationship documentation (Marriage Certificate / Legal Declaration).
   - Identity Verification: Government ID upload (Aadhaar/Passport/National ID) with biometric fingerprint/photo verification.
3. Legal Consents & Statutory Compliance:
   - Mandatory Electronic Consent Management: Signed digital informed consents for IVF, ICSI, Cryopreservation, PGT-A, Anesthesia, and Disposal/Donation of excess gametes.
   - ART Regulatory Board Compliance (e.g., National Assisted Reproductive Technology Act standards): Mandatory declarations, maximum allowed donor usage, ethical restrictions.
   - Witness and Counselor digital signatures with timestamp and tamper-proof PDF generation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 03: Clinical Consultation, Infertility Workup & Diagnostic Testing
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Purpose: In-depth clinical evaluation of both partners to diagnose male, female, or unexplained factors.
2. Female Infertility Workup:
   - History: Menstrual pattern, obstetrics history (gravida, para, abortions, ectopic), previous surgeries, laparoscopy history.
   - Ovarian Reserve Testing: Day 2/3 FSH, LH, Estradiol (E2), Serum AMH (Anti-Müllerian Hormone), TSH, Prolactin.
   - Pelvic Ultrasound: Baseline Antral Follicle Count (AFC), endometrial thickness, uterine anomalies, fibroids, adenomyosis, tubal patency (HSG / HyCoSy).
3. Male Infertility Workup (Andrology Clinic):
   - Detailed Semen Analysis (WHO guidelines): Volume, count, motility (progressive, non-progressive), morphology (Kruger strict criteria), vitality.
   - Advanced Andrology: Sperm DNA Fragmentation Index (DFI), Semen culture, hormonal profile (FSH, LH, Testosterone).
   - Surgical Sperm Retrieval evaluation (PESA / TESA / TESE / Micro-TESE) for azoospermic patients.
4. Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS):
   - Categorization: PCOS, Poor Ovarian Responder (POR/Bologna/POSEIDON criteria), Endometriosis (Stage I-IV), Male Factor, Recurrent Implantation Failure (RIF), Unexplained.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 04: Treatment Protocol Formulation & Ovarian Stimulation Monitoring
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Purpose: Plan medication protocols, monitor follicle progression, and pinpoint trigger timing.
2. Cycle Setup:
   - Treatment Type: IUI, Self-IVF, Self-ICSI, Donor Oocyte IVF, Freeze-All Cycle, FET.
   - Stimulation Protocol Choice:
     * Antagonist Protocol (Fixed or Flexible)
     * Long Agonist Protocol
     * Ultra-short / Flare Protocol
     * Progestin-primed Ovarian Stimulation (PPOS)
     * Mild IVF / Natural Cycle IVF
3. Folliculometry & Hormonal Tracking (Daily/Alternate Day Log):
   - Right Ovary Follicles (size in mm for each follicle e.g. R1: 18mm, R2: 16mm, R3: 14mm...)
   - Left Ovary Follicles (size in mm e.g. L1: 19mm, L2: 17mm...)
   - Endometrial Assessment: Thickness (mm) and pattern (Trilaminar / Hyperechoic / Homogenous).
   - Serum Hormones: E2 (Estradiol), P4 (Progesterone), LH.
4. Dynamic Drug Prescription & Injection Tracker:
   - Daily Gonadotropins (rFSH, hMG) dosage adjustments based on follicular growth curves.
   - Antagonist introduction trigger (Follicle >= 14mm or E2 thresholds).
5. Ovulation Trigger Planning:
   - Dual Trigger / hCG Trigger / GnRH Agonist Trigger (Decapeptyl/Lupron) timing.
   - Exact trigger timestamp countdown clock (OPU scheduled strictly 35 to 36 hours post-trigger).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 05: Oocyte Pick-Up (OPU), Andrology Lab & Semen Preparation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Purpose: Record surgical egg retrieval, husband/donor sperm collection, and wash preparation.
2. OPU Procedure Documentation:
   - Anesthesia notes, surgeon, embryologist present, needle gauge, aspiration pressure.
   - Total Cumulus-Oocyte Complexes (COCs) retrieved.
   - Follicular fluid inspection, flushing details, and immediate post-op patient recovery vitals.
3. Sperm Sample Management:
   - Fresh ejaculation / Thawed cryopreserved semen / PESA / TESA / Micro-TESE sample.
   - Witnessing double-check (Barcode & RFID tags to prevent sample mix-up).
   - Preparation Technique: Density Gradient Centrifugation, Swim-up, Microfluidic chip sorting (e.g., Zymōt).
   - Post-wash parameters: Final count, progressive motility yield, and readiness for insemination.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 06: Embryology Laboratory Information System (ELIS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Purpose: Maintain high-precision tracking of oocytes, fertilization, embryo development, and genetic testing.
2. Denudation & Oocyte Maturity Grading:
   - Stripping of cumulus cells post-OPU:
     * Metaphase II (MII - mature, with 1st polar body) -> Ready for ICSI
     * Metaphase I (MI - intermediate)
     * Germinal Vesicle (GV - immature)
     * Degenerated / Atretic
3. Fertilization Insemination:
   - Conventional IVF vs. ICSI vs. IMSI vs. PICSI.
   - Day 1 Fertilization Check (16-18 hours post-insemination):
     * 2PN (Normal diploid fertilization)
     * 0PN (Unfertilized)
     * 1PN / 3PN / Multi-PN (Abnormal fertilization)
4. Cleavage Stage Development (Day 2 & Day 3):
   - Cell count (2-cell, 4-cell, 8-cell), symmetry, fragmentation percentage (<10%, 10-25%, >25%), multinucleation.
5. Extended Culture & Blastocyst Grading (Day 5 & Day 6 - Gardner System):
   - Expansion stage: Grade 1 to 6 (Early, Cavitated, Expanded, Hatching, Hatched).
   - Inner Cell Mass (ICM): A (Tightly packed), B (Loosely grouped), C (Very few cells).
   - Trophectoderm (TE): A (Cohesive epithelium), B (Few cells), C (Sparse / degenerated).
   - Example tag: Day 5 - Embryo #3 - 4AA.
6. Advanced Technologies:
   - Time-Lapse Imaging (EmbryoScope) metadata integration.
   - Preimplantation Genetic Testing (PGT-A / PGT-M / PGT-SR): Biopsy log, trophectoderm cell sample shipment ID, genetic lab result import (Euploid / Aneuploid / Mosaic / Inconclusive).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 07: Embryo Transfer (ET) Management (Fresh & Frozen)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Fresh vs. Frozen Workflow:
   - Fresh ET: Performed Day 3 or Day 5 in the stimulated cycle.
   - Frozen Embryo Transfer (FET) Cycle:
     * Endometrial Preparation: Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT with Oral/Transdermal Estrogen) vs. Natural Cycle FET vs. Modified Natural.
     * Endometrial monitoring: Reaching optimal thickness (>8mm, trilaminar) before Progesterone start date.
     * ERA (Endometrial Receptivity Analysis) window-of-implantation timing sync.
2. Embryo Thawing Log (for FET):
   - Tank/Canister retrieval, thaw protocol verification, post-thaw survival rate (100%, 80%, degenerate).
   - Pre-transfer re-expansion check.
3. Transfer Procedure:
   - Selection of embryo(s) for transfer (Single Embryo Transfer - SET encouraged to avoid multiples).
   - Electronic witnessing verification at bedside (Doctor, Embryologist, Patient identity match).
   - Catheter type, ultrasound guidance confirmation, easy/difficult transfer, catheter flush check (retained embryo check negative).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 08: Luteal Phase Support & Early Pregnancy Outcome Tracking
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Luteal Phase Support Prescription:
   - Micronized Progesterone (Vaginal pessary / gel / IM Depot / Oral Dydrogesterone).
   - Adjuvant support: Low-dose aspirin, LMWH, Prednisolone, Vitamin D.
2. Biochemical Pregnancy Evaluation:
   - Serum Beta-hCG on Day 14 post-ET (or Day 16 post-D3 ET).
   - Result classification:
     * Negative (< 5 mIU/mL): Proceed to Cycle Failure Module.
     * Borderline (5 - 25 mIU/mL): Retest in 48 hours.
     * Positive (> 25 mIU/mL): Retest in 48 hours for exponential doubling (66%+ rise).
3. Clinical Pregnancy Confirmation (Ultrasound Scans):
   - 6th Week Gestational Scan: Identification of intrauterine gestational sac (GS), yolk sac, and exclusion of ectopic pregnancy.
   - 7th-8th Week Viability Scan: Measurement of Crown-Rump Length (CRL) and fetal cardiac activity (heartbeat detection).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 09: Cycle Failure Analysis, Root Cause Workup & Re-attempt Engine
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Purpose: Handle biochemical failure, implantation failure, early miscarriage, or poor lab yields, and systematically guide the couple into their next attempt.
2. Failure Type Categorization:
   - OPU Failure: Empty Follicle Syndrome (EFS), poor oocyte quality, low yield.
   - Fertilization Failure: Total fertilization failure (TFF), cleavage arrest before Day 3.
   - Blastulation Arrest: Failure to develop into usable blastocysts.
   - Implantation Failure: Negative Beta-hCG after transfer of good-quality embryos.
   - Biochemical Pregnancy Loss: Chemical pregnancy with falling hCG.
   - Clinical Miscarriage: Blighted ovum, early fetal demise (< 12 weeks).
3. Root Cause Analysis (RCA) & Multi-Disciplinary Review Board:
   - Post-failure debriefing questionnaire and review checklist.
   - Diagnostic recommendations triggered: Hysteroscopy (chronic endometritis, polyps, adhesions), Karyotyping of couple, Thrombophilia screen, Immunological panel, Endometrial Receptivity Array (ERA), Sperm DFI re-test.
4. Re-Attempt Strategy & Protocol Adaptation Engine:
   - Side-by-side comparison matrix: "Cycle 1 vs. Planned Cycle 2".
   - Protocol modification suggestions:
     * Switch from Agonist to Antagonist or vice-versa.
     * Alter gonadotropin brand (add recombinant LH / Luveris).
     * Add adjuvant therapies (Growth Hormone priming, DHEA, CoQ10, PRP therapy - Ovarian/Endometrial).
     * Switch from IVF to ICSI/IMSI, or employ PICSI / Calcium Ionophore activation.
     * Transition to Third-Party Reproduction (Donor Egg / Donor Sperm) when indicated.
   - Financial credit transfer / Discounted Package rollover for second attempts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 10: Antenatal Care (ANC) & High-Risk Obstetric Monitoring
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Transition of Care: Smooth handover from Reproductive Endocrinologist to Obstetrician/Gynecologist at 10-12 weeks.
2. ANC Clinical Tracking:
   - Calculation of Expected Date of Delivery (EDD) calibrated from exact embryo transfer date and embryonic age.
   - Trimester-by-Trimester Consultation Visits:
     * 1st Trimester: First Trimester Screening (FTS), NT/NB Scan, Non-Invasive Prenatal Testing (NIPT).
     * 2nd Trimester: Anomaly Scan (Level II / TIFFA scan), Cervical length monitoring (prevent cervical incompetence in IVF pregnancies), Fetal Echo.
     * 3rd Trimester: Fetal Growth Scans, Color Doppler (Umbilical and Middle Cerebral Artery pulsatility), Biophysical Profile (BPP).
   - High-Risk Screening & Alert Flags:
     * Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (GDM - OGTT tracking).
     * Pregnancy-Induced Hypertension (PIH / Preeclampsia - BP & urine protein logs).
     * Multiple gestation monitoring (Twins / Triplets chorionicity & amnionicity).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 11: Labor Room, Delivery Documentation & Birth Registry
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Admission & Labor Progression:
   - Admission for elective LSCS (common in IVF/elderly primigravida) or spontaneous/induced labor.
   - Digital Partogram: Cervical dilation, fetal descent, uterine contraction frequency, fetal heart rate monitoring (CTG).
2. Delivery Recording:
   - Mode of Delivery: Normal Vaginal Delivery, Assisted (Ventouse/Forceps), or Lower Segment Cesarean Section (LSCS).
   - Surgical / Procedural details, delivering obstetrician, pediatrician on call, anesthesia team.
   - Baby Arrival Record: Exact date and time of birth, gender, birth weight (grams), head circumference, length.
   - Immediate Neonatal Evaluation: APGAR Score at 1 minute and 5 minutes, resuscitation needs.
3. Multi-Fetal Delivery Splitting:
   - Auto-spawns independent newborn records linked to the mother (e.g., Twin Baby 1, Twin Baby 2) with individual hospital numbers.
4. Official Birth Documentation:
   - Birth registration forms, municipal authority exports, and clinic graduation certificate.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 12: Neonatal / Pediatric Care & Comprehensive Vaccination Scheduler
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Purpose: Provide pediatric care, growth milestone tracking, and a bulletproof immunization scheduler.
2. Immediate Postnatal Newborn Care:
   - Day 0 In-Hospital Immunization: BCG, Oral Polio Vaccine (OPV-0), Hepatitis B birth dose.
   - Newborn Screening (NBS): Congenital Hypothyroidism, G6PD deficiency, Hearing Screening (OAE/BERA), Critical Congenital Heart Disease (CCHD) pulse oximetry.
3. Automated Vaccination Scheduler:
   - Standard Protocols Supported: Universal Immunization Programme (UIP), IAP (Indian Academy of Pediatrics), CDC/WHO immunization schedules.
   - Vaccination Calendar Generation based on date of birth:
     * 6 Weeks: DTwP/DTaP, IPV, Hep B, Hib, Rotavirus, PCV (Pneumococcal).
     * 10 Weeks: 2nd dose of primary combination vaccines.
     * 14 Weeks: 3rd dose of primary combination vaccines.
     * 6 Months: Influenza (Flu), Typhoid Conjugate Vaccine.
     * 9 Months: MMR (Measles, Mumps, Rubella), Meningococcal.
     * 12 Months: Hepatitis A.
     * 15-18 Months: MMR booster, PCV booster, Varicella (Chickenpox).
     * 2 Years & Beyond: Annual flu, Typhoid booster, DTaP/IPV school boosters.
4. Vaccination Operations:
   - Vaccine vial batch/lot tracking, expiry verification, cold-chain temperature log.
   - Automated SMS/WhatsApp immunization reminders sent to parents 7 days and 1 day prior to due date.
   - Adverse Event Following Immunization (AEFI) logging.
   - Downloadable/Printable International Standard Pediatric Immunization Certificate.
5. Pediatric Growth & Milestone Tracker:
   - WHO Growth Charts (Weight-for-age, Length/Height-for-age, Head Circumference-for-age percentiles).
   - Milestone checklist: Motor, cognitive, speech, and social milestones up to 5 years.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 13: Cryobank & Bio-Repository Management
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Purpose: Physical and digital traceability of high-value gametes and embryos with zero tolerance for confusion.
2. Grid Storage Hierarchy:
   - Cryo Room -> Liquid Nitrogen (LN2) Tank ID -> Canister Number -> Goblet ID -> Cane Number -> Straw/Vial ID -> Color Code.
3. Specimen Types Tracked:
   - Oocytes (Vitrified MII eggs).
   - Sperm (Donor sperm vials, husband autologous sperm, testicular tissue fragments).
   - Embryos (Day 3 cleavage embryos, Day 5/6 blastocysts).
4. Cryo Operations:
   - Vitrification / Freezing log with embryologist signature.
   - Thawing / Disposal / Donation log.
   - Automatic Storage Fee Expiry & Renewal Invoicing: Annual recurring maintenance invoices sent to couples with automated reminder cycles.
   - Tank Temperature & LN2 level telemetry integration (IoT sensor alarms for temperature drop).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 14: Third-Party Reproduction / Donor & Surrogacy Protocol
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Regulatory Adherence: Compliant with national ART laws governing altruistic vs. commercial procedures, donor anonymity, and registry limits.
2. Oocyte Donor Registry:
   - Donor KYC, physical characteristics (height, eye color, hair color, skin tone), blood group, education, karyotype, infectious disease screening (HIV, HBsAg, HCV, VDRL).
   - Donation cycle history: Enforcing statutory limit on maximum lifetime donations.
3. Sperm Bank Integration:
   - Quarantine tracking (6-month quarantine and repeat infectious disease test before release).
4. Gestational Surrogacy Management (Where permitted by law):
   - Legal contracts, medical clearances, clinical monitoring of surrogate mother, and escrow payment milestone tracking.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 15: Fertility Pharmacy, Cold-Chain & Consumables Inventory
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Specialized Fertility Formulary:
   - Gonadotropins (Gonal-F, Puregon, Menopur), GnRH agonists/antagonists (Cetrotide, Decapeptyl), Progesterone varieties.
2. Cold Chain Management (2°C - 8°C):
   - Fridge temperature monitoring logs and alerts to prevent loss of sensitive protein hormones.
3. Embryology Lab Consumables:
   - Culture media (Sequential vs. Single-step media), oil overlay, aspiration needles, ICSI holding and injection pipettes, transfer catheters.
   - Batch/Lot expiry warnings and QA certificates per batch.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 16: Billing, Treatment Packages, Installments & Financing
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Package Configurator:
   - Pre-packaged cycle costs (e.g., "Self-ICSI Package includes OPU, ICSI, and Day 3 ET; excludes freezing and pharmacy").
   - Add-on services: PGT-A per embryo, IMSI, Zymōt, Laser Assisted Hatching (LAH), Embryo Glue.
2. Multi-Stage Payment Installments:
   - Milestone 1: Stimulation Start (Advance payment)
   - Milestone 2: Day of OPU (Laboratory charge)
   - Milestone 3: Day of Embryo Transfer (Procedure charge)
   - Milestone 4: Cryopreservation & Maintenance (Annual storage charge)
3. Financial Counseling & EMI Options:
   - Healthcare zero-cost EMI partner integration, medical loan approvals.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 17: Clinical Quality, ART Registry Reporting & Lab KPI Analytics
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. National & International ART Registry Exports:
   - One-click structured export for Government ART registries and ESHRE/SART benchmarks.
2. Embryology Quality KPIs:
   - Insemination-to-Fertilization Rate (Target: > 75-80% in ICSI).
   - Day 3 Cleavage Rate (Target: > 95%).
   - Day 5 Blastulation Rate (Target: > 45-60%).
   - Post-Thaw Embryo Survival Rate (Target: > 90%).
3. Clinical Outcomes:
   - Clinical Pregnancy Rate (CPR) per transfer.
   - Implantation Rate (IR).
   - Multiple Gestation Rate (MGR) (Aiming for low rate via Single Embryo Transfer).
   - Take-Home Baby Rate / Live Birth Rate (LBR) per started cycle and per transfer.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 18: Couple Interactive Portal & Mobile Application
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Patient Empowered Features:
   - Interactive Medication & Injection Timetable with alarm reminders and video injection guides.
   - Live cycle tracking view: Follicular growth charts and lab report access.
   - High-definition embryo photos / videos (post-blastocyst stage) approved by embryologist.
   - Digital e-signing of consents without standing in queues.
   - Pregnancy week-by-week tracker and child vaccination schedule with push notifications.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE 19: Security, Multi-Role Access Control (RBAC) & Audit Trails
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Role-Based Permissions:
   - Roles: Super Admin, Clinic Director, Reproductive Endocrinologist, Embryologist, Andrologist, Fertility Nurse, Pharmacist, Billing Executive, Receptionist, Patient.
2. Segregation of Duties:
   - Embryologists only modify ELIS and Cryo records.
   - Financial teams cannot edit clinical notes.
   - Doctors sign off on treatment protocols and triggers.
3. Security & Traceability:
   - 21 CFR Part 11 and HIPAA compliant electronic audit logs: every edit, deletion, or viewing of medical records is timestamped with user ID and IP address.
   - End-to-end encryption for sensitive patient and genetic data.

PART 5: IMPLEMENTATION PHASES & ROADMAP
================================================================================

Phase 1: Foundation, CRM & Clinical Diagnostics
- Setup couple database, role-based access control, and electronic consent engine.
- Implement patient enquiry CRM and pre-IVF infertility diagnostic workup.

Phase 2: Stimulation, OPU & Embryology Lab Suite (ELIS)
- Follicular monitoring (dynamic follicle input UI with endometrium tracking).
- OPU and Semen preparation modules.
- Complete Embryology Lab System: Insemination, fertilization, Day 3 & Day 5 grading, and Cryo-tank inventory.

Phase 3: Embryo Transfer, Outcomes & Failure/Re-attempt Engine
- Fresh ET and FET modules with electronic witnessing checklist.
- Beta-hCG tracking, ultrasound viability confirmation.
- Cycle failure classification, root-cause investigation forms, and comparative cycle 2 protocol generator.

Phase 4: Obstetric Care (ANC), Delivery & Pediatric Immunization
- Transition to ANC module with risk stratifications and trimester scans.
- Labor room documentation, Cesarean/normal delivery registry.
- Newborn registry and automated pediatric vaccination tracker with SMS/WhatsApp alerts.

Phase 5: Financials, Patient App & Regulatory Compliance
- Billing packages, installment tracking, and cryo-storage renewals.
- Patient Mobile App (injection calendar, embryo photo viewer, consent e-sign).
